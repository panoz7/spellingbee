Spelling Bee Bookmarklets

A collection of bookmarklets that provide interesting information while you solve the NY Times Spelling Bee puzzle. Some give you the answers. Others give you clues. Have fun!

    How to Install

    The easy way

    Drag and drop the bookmarklet link into your browser's bookmarks bar or bookmarks sidebar.

    The slightly harder way

    1. Click the bookmarklet link to copy the bookmarklet code into your clipboard.
    2. Bookmark this page, noting where you're saving it. Rename the bookmark using the name of the bookmarklet you're creating.
    3. Open your browsers bookmarks manager, find the bookmark, right click on it, and edit it.
    4. Paste the bookmarklet content into the URL field.
